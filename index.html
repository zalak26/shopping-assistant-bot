About the Project

ShoppingAssistant is a shopping assistant project designed to help users discover products based on their requirements and preferences.

Currently under development

What It Will Do

  • Help users search for products.
  • Provide relevant product recommendations.
  • Help users compare available options.
  • Provide links to products on supported online marketplaces.

How It Works

Users will be able to tell the bot what type of product they are looking for. The bot will use those requirements to help identify relevant products and provide useful information to assist with product discovery.
